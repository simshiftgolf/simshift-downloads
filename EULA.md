## 6. How AutoShift works

AutoShift runs on your computer, reads local network traffic from supported golf simulation software to determine a golfer's handedness, and sends move commands to your Rail over your home network (or through your Arduino Cloud account if you choose that mode). The application requires administrator permission on Windows to read that traffic. You are responsible for running it only on computers you are authorized to use.
